GAME RULES

  • Starting Bankroll: You start with 50 Points. Choose your wager and capacity before loading each round.
  • Chamber Loading: Choose between 3 to 8 shell capacity. Each shell has a 2/5 (40%) chance of being Live.
  • Blank Self-Shot Bonus: If you shoot yourself with a BLANK, you take 0 damage and receive an EXTRA BONUS TURN!
  • Pot Accumulation: Shooting the Dealer with a Live round adds points to the Pending Pot based on your capacity multiplier.
  • Live Shot Penalty: If you get shot by a Live round (either by yourself or the Dealer), points are deducted from the Pending Pot at a heightened penalty rate (1.25x the multiplier gain rate).
  • Claiming Points: The Pot is only awarded to your bankroll when the Dealer is eliminated!
  • Endless Rounds: Rounds continue seamlessly until HP hits 0. Eliminating the Dealer restores full health for the next round.
